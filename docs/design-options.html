ScamShield: one screen, six design styles

The same "check a suspicious message" card drawn in each style, with how well it suits a scam checker used by everyday citizens and how it adds to your portfolio.

Recommendation: neo-brutalism for ScamShield

A scam checker has one job: make danger unmissable for anyone, including parents and grandparents on a cheap phone. Neo-brutalism's thick borders, flat bright colours and hard shadows give the strongest contrast, read well in Hindi and Marathi, cost nothing in performance, and give your portfolio a visibly different third style next to TRINETRA (neumorphic) and VaaniShield/NyayaSathi (cyber HUD).
